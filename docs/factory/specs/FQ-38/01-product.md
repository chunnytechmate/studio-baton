# FQ-38 — Product outcome

## The owner's problem

The owner cannot stay in a productive feature-building flow because routine factory work
repeatedly stops for small handoff decisions. Waiting and bookkeeping consume much more
time than implementation, while a completed safety label can still disagree with the
repository's final checks. The owner is paying the attention cost of a strict factory
without consistently receiving its promised enforcement.

## Measurable success

- An approved, cohesive feature can reach its final acceptance point without intermediate
  owner intervention by default.
- A cohesive feature asks for no more than two deliberate owner decisions: approve its
  direction when needed, then accept or reject the completed result.
- Work is never presented as ready while any required repository check is failing.
- Routine audit activity does not request an immediate owner decision.
- High-risk work, changes to trusted evidence, real learner data, and real human messaging
  retain their explicit human boundaries.
- The owner can see the true pending-decision count, including every open result that needs
  a merge or close decision.

## Shipped announcement

Studio Baton's factory now protects the dangerous edges without interrupting every safe
step. Approved features flow to one final review, readiness agrees with the repository's
checks, and routine audit evidence no longer fills the owner's merge queue.

## Deliberately not doing

- We are not allowing an agent to make the final merge decision.
- We are not weakening protection around real learner data or real outbound messages.
- We are not allowing agents to rewrite trusted tests or safety policy without explicit
  owner approval.
- We are not removing independent review or negative-test evidence.
- We are not changing Studio Baton product behavior, preparing a release, or upgrading
  dependencies.
- We are not promising zero owner attention; we are concentrating it at decisions that
  genuinely require judgment.
