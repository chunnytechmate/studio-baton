# FQ-52 — Truthful bootstrap evidence

## User problem

The studio owner approved a cautious recovery process, but the process stops even when the
proposed change is safe because one proof requirement cannot be satisfied as written. The
owner needs recovery to continue without pretending an impossible proof passed and without
personally reconstructing the evidence chain.

## Measurable success

- A recovery attempt can produce one complete, truthful evidence package for the exact
  change awaiting review.
- Incomplete, stale, conflicting, or circular evidence always stops the attempt visibly.
- The already-approved baseline repair can resume without asking the owner to approve a
  different behavioral change.
- Later recovery work remains blocked until the baseline repair is reviewed, accepted, and
  confirmed healthy.

## Announcement

Studio Baton’s recovery lane can now prove its work without circular evidence. The owner
receives one reviewable package tied to the exact change, while missing or mismatched proof
still stops the process automatically.

## Deliberately not doing

- Changing Studio Baton’s learner, lesson, song, calendar, media, or messaging behavior.
- Weakening independent review, automated checks, or the owner’s final decision.
- Expanding recovery to additional work while the baseline remains unresolved.
- Replacing or broadening the already-approved baseline formatting change.
- Treating a partial or provisional result as complete.
